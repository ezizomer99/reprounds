# Delete Your RepRounds Account

You can permanently delete your RepRounds account and all associated data directly from within the app.

## Steps

1. Open the **RepRounds** app
2. Tap the **Profile** tab
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**
5. Confirm twice when prompted

## What gets deleted

- Your account and login credentials
- All workout sessions and training history
- Custom exercises and disciplines
- Body weight entries
- All other personal data stored in RepRounds

Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
